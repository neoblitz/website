Hello, I'm Arun.

I am a cybersecurity researcher and engineer with over twenty years of experience protecting critical systems — from spacecraft and smart grids to the collaboration tools millions of people use every day. Currently a Principal Security Researcher at Microsoft working at intersection of cybersecurity and AI; previously led cyber defense engineering and research at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory.

I am passionate about using technology to solve challenging problems and making an impact. I am excited about the new opportunities opened up by advances in AI, and have been actively exploring its potential across domains, especially in cybersecurity and healthcare. While I remain committed to advancing the field of cybersecurity, I am also dedicated to sharing knowledge through writing and speaking, and mentoring the next generation of talent.
